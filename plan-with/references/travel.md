# Travel rules for plan-with

Read this in Step 6. The numbers below are starting points. Always confirm times and fares with a live lookup
on the day of the plan, and say "about" when a number is an estimate.

## Look-ups

- Transit: Google Maps directions in transit mode, opened in the browser (no key needed), with the departure
  set to the plan's start time. For the trip home, set the departure to the event's end time and check that a
  trip exists. Valley Metro's trip planner (https://www.valleymetro.org) is the backup.
- Driving: Google Maps driving time at that hour. Note the parking: free street parking, a garage, or an ASU
  structure (look up the price for that evening).
- ASU shuttles (intercampus and Tempe routes): check the current schedule on ASU's parking and transit site
  before relying on one. Many don't run late or on weekends.

## Rules, in order

1. Everyone needs a way there and a way back. "Back" means the return trip still runs when the event ends,
   plus 15 minutes of slack. If the last train or bus leaves before then, the plan fails for that person
   unless they can carpool home.
2. Nobody in the group has a car: keep only plans reachable by transit, walking (up to about 20 minutes, less
   in heat or late at night) or an ASU shuttle.
3. Uber-averse people (profile says Uber OK = no): drop any plan where they're the only one who'd need an Uber.
   Let them share a ride with someone who's going anyway only if their profile doesn't forbid Uber entirely.
4. Pairing: a driver with free seats picks up a friend when the detour is under about 10 minutes. Two
   transit riders whose homes are on the same line meet at a stop and ride together. Say who meets whom, where.
5. Split arrivals: when overlap.py gives a "joins late" window, plan the group's trip from the start time and
   the late person's trip from where they'll be (class building, work), arriving at their earliest time.
6. Night safety: after dark, prefer routes with short walks from the stop, and pair people up when possible.

## Cost per person

Add up each person's round trip and the event cost.

- Valley Metro light rail, bus and streetcar: check the current fare. It's been about $2 a ride, or a $4 day
  pass, which is cheaper for a round trip with a transfer. Students with a transit pass (ask once and save
  it in their profile) pay $0. The Tempe Streetcar and Tempe's Orbit neighborhood shuttles have been free.
- ASU shuttles: free for students.
- Driving: miles round trip × current Arizona gas price ÷ 25 mpg, plus parking. Split it evenly across
  everyone in the car, unless the student says the driver covers it.
- Uber or Lyft: look up an estimate for that time and split it across the riders.

Show one total per person ("$9 each, $4 of it light rail") and keep it under each person's budget.
